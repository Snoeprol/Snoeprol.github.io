**Minder gedoe. Meer winst.** — for transport companies that would rather plan on data than
on gut feel.

DataFirst Logistics takes a fleet, a day's shipments and the real road network and produces
routes that are genuinely optimized: fewer kilometres driven, tighter delivery windows, and
a live timeline a planner can actually work with. The gains are real and measured — 15–30%
less distance on the routes it plans — because the optimizer is solving the actual vehicle
routing problem against real driving times, not drawing straight lines on a map.

### The shape of it

- **Real routing, not estimates.** Distances and drive times come from the true road
  network, so a plan that looks good on screen holds up on the road.
- **One platform, planning to reporting.** Build the plan, dispatch it, and see afterward
  what actually happened against what was planned.
- **Built for the operation.** Delivery windows, vehicle capacities, depots and the
  awkward real-world constraints that make routing hard rather than a textbook exercise.

**Stack** — a vehicle-routing core over OSRM road-network data, a web planning app, and an
Astro marketing site. Deployed and in use.
