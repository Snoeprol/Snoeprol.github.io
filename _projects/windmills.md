A working application for offshore wind: it ingests turbine and site datasets, reconciles
their quirks (identifiers that are not as unique as they look, sources that barely change
between releases), and presents them on a map you can actually navigate. Built and deployed
on its own server.

A smaller, quieter project than the others here, but a good example of the same instinct:
take messy real-world reference data and make it something a person can trust at a glance.
